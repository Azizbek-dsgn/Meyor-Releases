Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers Hustler and the apps it installs (Swible, Swible Lab, Voxly, Tempass), made by Meyor Vision. In short: everything runs on your own computer, and we do not run servers that receive your data.

Sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you choose “Google bilan kirish”, Google’s own sign-in page opens in your browser and sends the result directly back to Hustler on your computer. Hustler requests only the basic scopes openid, email and profile and keeps only:

  • your name,
  • your email address,
  • your profile picture,
  • your Google account ID.

These are stored in a file on your computer and are used only to show who is signed in and to link your own computers to one account. Hustler does not store Google access or refresh tokens, does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and does not send your profile to Meyor Vision or anyone else. Signing out deletes this information from the computer.

Hustler’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your videos, audio and text

Subtitles, transcription (Swible, Swible Lab) and dictation (Voxly) run on your computer with local speech models. Your media, recordings and text are not uploaded. Voxly keeps its history and statistics on your computer only.

AI assistants (Claude, Codex)

If you connect Claude or Codex to Swible, the AI app you use talks to Swible on your computer. What you send to that AI is governed by its provider’s own privacy policy (Anthropic or OpenAI). Meyor Vision does not receive it.

Network use

Hustler connects to GitHub to check for and download updates, and the apps download speech models from Hugging Face the first time. These requests contain no personal information beyond what any download includes (such as your IP address, seen by those services).

Sharing between your computers

Future versions may sync your settings between computers signed in to the same account, directly computer to computer and end-to-end encrypted. No central server of ours will hold your data.

Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the apps change how they handle data. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Azizbek-dsgn/Meyor-Releases/issues.
