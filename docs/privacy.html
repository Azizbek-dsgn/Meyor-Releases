Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers Hustler and the apps it installs (Swible, Swible Lab, Voxly, Tempass), made by Meyor Vision. In short: your videos, audio and text never leave your computer. Your account record is encrypted so that only the Meyor Vision administrator can read it, and it is stored on GitHub — we run no server of our own.

Account (Sign in with Google)

Hustler asks you to sign in with Google and to choose a username. Google’s own sign-in page opens in your browser and sends the result directly back to Hustler on your computer. Hustler requests only openid, email and profile. It does not store Google access or refresh tokens and does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

What is kept, and where:

  • On your computer: your name, email, profile picture, Google account ID and username.
  • Account record (encrypted): the same profile, your username, your computer’s operating system, the versions of Meyor apps installed and when they were last used, and a random ID for each of your computers. Hustler encrypts this record with the administrator’s public key (X25519 + AES-256-GCM) before sending it, and stores it in a private GitHub repository. Only the administrator’s key — kept on the administrator’s own computer and in that repository’s automation secrets — can decrypt it. GitHub sees only encrypted text.
  • Proof of account: the first record from a computer includes the signed ID token Google returned at sign-in, so that nobody can pretend to be you. It is checked once and then no longer sent or kept.
  • Public, signed by the administrator: the list of usernames with a pseudonymous account code (a hash, not your email), blocked account codes with the reason, and required updates. Apps read this list to show your username and to apply blocks and required updates.

The administrator uses this only to run the apps: to see how many people use them and which versions, to block accounts that break the rules, to require important updates, and to review the skill market. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Required updates: important updates can be made mandatory; Hustler then installs them automatically.

Deleting your account: Hustler → Settings → “Akkauntni o‘chirish” removes your record, username and published skills on the next processing run (within about an hour).

Hustler’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Skill market

If you publish a skill, it becomes public in the Swible-Skills repository under your username, with the description, tags and optional donation link you entered, after the administrator approves it. A donation link opens an outside site; any payment is between you and the author.

Your videos, audio and text

Subtitles, transcription (Swible, Swible Lab) and dictation (Voxly) run on your computer with local speech models. Your media, recordings and text are not uploaded. Voxly keeps its history and statistics on your computer only.

AI assistants

If you connect Claude, Codex or Google Antigravity to Swible, the AI app you use talks to Swible on your computer. What you send to that AI is governed by its provider’s own privacy policy (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google). Meyor Vision does not receive it. The free local AI (Ollama) runs entirely on your computer.

Network use

Hustler connects to GitHub to check for and download updates, and the apps download speech models from Hugging Face the first time. These requests contain no personal information beyond what any download includes (such as your IP address, seen by those services).

Sharing between your computers

Settings sync uses a password-encrypted file you keep yourself (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, a USB stick). It never contains your account or keys.

Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact
