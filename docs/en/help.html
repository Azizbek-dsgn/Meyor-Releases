Help

Mac says “Hustler Not Opened”

Normal for free apps without a paid certificate. System Settings → Privacy & Security → “Open Anyway”. Pictures on the install page.

Windows says “isn't commonly downloaded” or “protected your PC”

In Edge: ⋯ → Keep → Keep anyway; in SmartScreen: More info → Run anyway.

Swible does not show in Premiere

Check Swible is installed in Hustler, then restart Premiere: Window → Extensions → Swible.

The AI does not connect

Hustler → Settings → AI assistant: “Install”, then “Sign in”, then “Connect”.

Make auto montage follow my style

Write your rules in a folder with a SKILL.md file and put it in Documents (or Hustler → Skills). Swible’s AI reads them by itself.

Tempass does not record time

Keep the Swible panel open in Premiere/After Effects with a saved project, and automatic tracking on in Tempass.

Where is my data?

Only on your computer. To move it: Hustler → Settings → Sync.
